In-window overlays stay in the retained scene. Operating-system dialogs use platform prompts.

## Popover

Compose `Popover` trigger, positioner, popup, and arrow parts. The core owns placement, collisions, focus, outside interaction, and dismissal. Store `open` on the view.

## Dialog

`Dialog::new` and `Dialog::alert` add modal semantics plus title, description, backdrop, viewport, and close parts. Restore focus through `restore_focus_to`; do not install a second focus trap.

## System popover

`SystemPopover` owns a separate native panel anchored to a mounted node. It can cross the parent’s content bounds and is disposed with its owner.

## Native dialogs

```rust
cx.prompt("Save changes?", ["Save", "Cancel"]);
cx.prompt_for_paths(PathPromptOptions::new().files(true).multiple(true));
```
